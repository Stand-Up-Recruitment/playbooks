SUR Cold Email Templates

Every cold email and text for reaching candidates, in one place. Click a template in the left panel to view it.

JobAdder candidates get the 4-email sequence: 4 emails over 12 days, plain text, no links or images. Trade Me candidates get the role-specific email or text.

Run rules for the JobAdder sequence
• Any reply stops the sequence for that person.
• Answer a "yes" or "1" within 1 hour, between 7am and 7pm.
• A "2" gets their Notice Period set 3 months out, then goes back in at Email 4.
• A "3" or "stop" gets removed from the list and their status updated.
• For past candidates, swap the first line of Email 1 for: "You registered with us back in {year} when you were looking at Aus."
