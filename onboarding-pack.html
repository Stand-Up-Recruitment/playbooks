Xero Me: your pay & leave app

Xero Me is where you'll see your payslips, submit timesheets, check your leave balance and apply for leave, all from your phone.

Do this on Day 1

Get set up

  1. Check your personal email. We've sent you a Xero Me invite.
  2. Click the link and accept the invite.
  3. Create your profile with your email and a password. Keep these handy.
  4. Download Xero Me from the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).
  5. Log in with the profile details you just created.
No invite? Check your junk or spam folder first, then let Nihanga know.

What you can do in Xero Me

🧾
View your payslipsA new one every Wednesday.
🕒
Submit your timesheetsWeekly. See Timesheets.
📊
Check your leave balanceSee how much leave you have available.
🌴
Apply for leaveAnnual and sick leave go through Xero Me.

Step-by-step guides

  1. Open Xero Me and tap Leave.
  2. Tap Request leave (or the + button).
  3. Choose the leave type, e.g. Annual Leave or Sick Leave.
  4. Select your start and end dates and check the hours.
  5. Add a short description and tap Submit.
Applying in Xero Me is only one part of the process. Check Leave & absences for notice periods and who to tell.
  1. Open Xero Me and tap Leave.
  2. Your available balance shows for each leave type.
  3. Tap a leave type to see your upcoming and past requests.
  1. Open Xero Me and tap Payslips.
  2. Tap any pay period to see the full breakdown.
  3. New payslips appear every Wednesday.

How we work

Timesheets

In shortSubmit your timesheet in Xero Me once the week ends: on Friday, or by Monday of the following week at the latest.

How to submit your timesheet

  1. Open Xero Me and tap Timesheets.
  2. Tap New timesheet (or +) and select the week.
  3. Enter your hours worked each day, Monday to Friday.
  4. Check your totals. Don't include your 30-minute unpaid lunch.
  5. Tap Submit. Or tap Save to keep a draft and submit later.

Get it right first time

📅
DeadlineFriday when the week ends, or Monday at the latest.
⏱️
Late start or early finish?Record your actual hours worked.
🌴
On leave?Leave you've applied for in Xero Me shows on your timesheet. Don't enter those hours as work.
💸
Why it mattersLate timesheets can delay your pay.

Frequently asked questions

I haven't received my Xero Me invite. What do I do?

Check your personal email's junk or spam folder. If it's not there, let Nihanga know and we'll resend it.

I forgot to submit my timesheet on Friday.

Submit it by Monday of the following week. Late timesheets can delay your pay, so try not to miss Monday.

When do I get paid?

Every week. Pay runs on Wednesday, your payslip arrives on Wednesday, and the money reaches your bank by Thursday.

Can I apply for birthday leave in Xero Me?

No. Birthday leave isn't applied through Xero Me. Email Nihanga at least 5 working days before.

I'm sick. Who do I tell?

Text Nihanga at least 1 hour before your start time (or Rachel if Nihanga's away), then apply for sick leave in Xero Me once you're back. If you're off for 3 or more consecutive days, a medical certificate is required.

How do I connect my phone to the Wi-Fi?

Network: SPARK-VEG53K. Password: TameStarfishR38%. Your laptop is already connected.

How do I print?

The printer is already set up on your laptop. Choose "Brother MFC-L2770DW series" when you print.

Where do I park?

Office parking is limited. Park for free on Rangitira Avenue, Takapuna, a short walk to the office.

Where can I find our policies?

SUR HUB › HR Policy Manual. Your leave entitlements are in your employment agreement and the Leave Policy.
