Your day

Two emails land every morning. They are your call list. Work them in this order.

  1. Before 8:00

    Team meeting, then admin

    Send the morning reminder text to everyone booked for a Teams call today. Tidy up anything left from yesterday so you can ring without stopping.

  2. 8:00 to 2:00

    Sourcing

    Ring the new candidates email. These are fresh people. Screen them and book anyone who’s ready.

  3. 2:00 to home time

    Nurturing

    Ring the bucket movers email. These are people whose move date is getting close. Find out where they’re at and book anyone who’s now ready. You’re done when the list is done.

  4. Lists finished early?

    Send a cold email

    Email past candidates in your trades. See Cold email.

Make the call

You don’t dial. JobAdder rings you, then connects the candidate. Every call is recorded, so don’t take notes. Just talk.

  1. Open the candidate in JobAdder.
  2. Hover over the status under their name and pick Screening.
  3. Your phone rings. Answer it and press any key.
  4. The candidate’s phone rings. You’re connected.
Watch: making a call

Do they tick the boxes?

  • They’re keen to move to Australia
  • They’re qualified in their trade
  • You know when they can move
  • You know where in Australia they want to go, or they’re open to anywhere
Screening call script

“Hey [Name]! It’s [Your Name] from Stand Up Recruitment. You showed interest in work in Australia, is that right?”

Questions

1. Why move: “What’s making you want to make the move to Australia?”

2. Skills and experience: “I can see that you are a [trade]. Can you expand a bit more on your skills and experience?”

3. Readiness to move: “And when are you looking to make the move?”

4. Where: “Where in Australia are you looking to go, or are you not too worried?”

5. Qualifications: “Are you qualified in that trade?” or “Do you hold a current [specific ticket]?”

6. Anything to add: “Great! Is there anything else you would like us to know or anything you would like to add?”

Don’t get into pay or job details. That’s the placer’s job on the Teams call. If they ask, say:

“Hey, look, my coworker will go through that with you on the call and see what jobs line up with your skills and experience.”

Close: suitable

“Sweet! It sounds like you’d be a really good fit for what we’ve got on at the moment. The next step is a proper chat with [Placer Name], one of our recruiters. They’ll run you through the role in detail, the pay, where it’s based and how the move over works, and they can answer any questions you’ve got. It’s a video call on Teams and takes about 30 minutes. Are you free [today / tomorrow] at [time]?”

Lock in a time before you hang up.

“Perfect, I’ve booked you in with [Placer Name] for [day] at [time]. You’ll get an email with the Teams link shortly, and I’ll send you a text reminder on the morning. Have a think about any questions you’ve got about the move. Talk soon!”

Close: not ready yet

“No worries, that’s really common. When you’re ready we can have a job sorted before you even leave NZ, with accommodation sorted and a free flight. I’ll check in closer to [month].”

Close: not suitable

“Awesome, thanks so much for chatting with me today [Name]. We will be in touch if anything comes up that matches your experience. Have a good one!”

How to book the Teams call in JobAdder
  1. Agree a day and time while they’re still on the phone. Today or tomorrow is best.
  2. Open the job card for their trade and find the candidate.
  3. Move them to the Phone Interview stage.
  4. Click the three dots beside their name, then Schedule Event, then Interview.
  5. Fill in the date, start time and end time.
  6. Under “Looks like you are scheduling a…”, pick Internal Meeting.
  7. Click Save Event. JobAdder emails the candidate for you.
  8. Change their status to In Process.
Never add the client to this meeting. It’s just us and the candidate.

Cold email
  1. Go to the Candidates tab in JobAdder.
  2. Filter Working Rights to NZ Citizen.
  3. Filter Categories to the trade.
  4. Select everyone in the list (50 at a time, max).
  5. Click Send Message, then Email the Candidate, then Send an Ad Hoc Email.
  6. Paste the subject and body from the template. Click Send.
