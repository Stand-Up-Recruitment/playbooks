Overview

Foundation rules, system setup, automation flow, and check-in calendar. Every Support Team member must know this section before running a placement.

Non-Negotiables
1 · Nothing moves until TOB is signed AND invoice is paid
No relocation work begins before this gate is cleared. No exceptions.
2 · Accommodation is booked before flights — always
Accommodation can fall through. Flights are easy and go every day. Never book flights first.
3 · Support Team owns the relationship post-handover
Recruiter is out. Any attempt to re-engage the recruiter goes back to Support Team. No exceptions.
4 · Every check-in must be logged in JobAdder with a health score
If it is not logged, it did not happen. This protects SUR if a placement is ever disputed.
5 · Flights booked within week 1 of placement — 3 weeks before start date
All candidates have 4 weeks from placement to start. Clients need flight confirmation as proof of commitment.
6 · Candidate must arrive at least 3 days before start date
Non-negotiable for both pathways. Communicate this clearly on the Documents Call.

Automation & System Overview
Your role starts at Step 3. Everything from Trigger through Step 2 is fully automated. When the "New Placement Notice" lands — that is your signal to begin.
Trigger
Recruiter submits candidate form in JobAdder
Automation wakes up. No manual trigger needed.
Step 1
Candidate record created in Airtable
Data verified across Airtable, Xero, and JobAdder.
Step 2
Invoice plan selected, Xero contact and invoice generated
Adroit, NEA, General 1, or General 2 — drives the full milestone schedule.
Step 3
Two emails fire + New Placement Notice to Support Team ★ You start here
Email 1 to candidate + alert to Support Team. Send Email 2B to client same day.
Step 4
Invoice email to candidate (Pathway B only)
Invoice + ASB bank details sent to candidate automatically.
Step 5
Candidate signs and pays
DocuSeal → Signed. Xero reconciliation → Paid. These don't always happen in the same order.
Step 6
Client pays invoice
Xero reconciles, client record updates, recruiter commission triggered.
Do not manually update Signed or Paid in Airtable. These are driven by automation. Manual edits corrupt payroll on next sync. Raise anything incorrect with Nihanga.

Difficult Candidate Protocol — Pre-Departure
Use this protocol any time a candidate is unresponsive, uncooperative, or raising concerns that aren't resolving in the normal flow. Every case must be logged in JobAdder.
1
Support Team resolves firstYou — attempt resolution
Attempt to resolve the issue directly. Call, text, email in that order. Give the candidate space to talk. Document the issue and your response in JobAdder as you go.
2
Escalate to assigned recruiter — if unresolved within 24–48 hoursEscalate → Ian or Ayn
If the issue is not resolved within 24–48 hours, escalate to the candidate's assigned recruiter (Ian or Ayn) the same day. Brief them fully — what happened, what you tried, what's outstanding. They take ownership from here. Continue to support but do not work around them.
3
Escalate to Les — if still unresolved after recruiter involvementFinal escalation → Les
If the recruiter cannot resolve it, escalate to Les directly. Do not delay — if the placement is at risk, Les needs to know same day. Log everything: dates, what was tried, who was involved, what the outstanding issue is.

Trade Licensing — NZ to Australia

State-by-state licensing requirements for NZ tradespeople working in Australia. TTMRA is not automatic — candidates must formally apply before starting work.

Required Documents — All Applications
Proof of identity (passport, driver's licence, birth certificate — 100 points of ID)
Current NZ licence — must be valid at time of application
2 recent passport photos
Unique Student Identifier (USI) — free at usi.gov.au
Payment method (fees vary by state)
Certified copies where required (JP or solicitor)
National Requirements — All States
Required for all sites
White Card (CPCCWHS1001)
Required for everyone on an Australian construction site. NZ Site Safe cards are NOT accepted.
Steps: 1. Create USI at usi.gov.au · 2. Find RTO at training.gov.au or complete online at whitecardonline.com.au · 3. Complete CPCCWHS1001 — 1 day in-person or a few hours online (~$100-150) · 4. Verify identity with RTO · 5. White Card posted within a few weeks. Valid for life, recognised in all states.
Applicable trades
High Risk Work Licences (HRWL)
Required for: Scaffolding, Rigging, Cranes, Forklifts, EWPs, Pressure Equipment. Nationally recognised.
Steps: 1. Locate NZ qualification certificate for relevant class · 2. Find Australian RTO at training.gov.au · 3. Book skills assessment · 4. Complete any gap training required · 5. RTO submits application to state regulator.
Trans-Tasman Mutual Recognition (TTMRA)
Legal agreement allowing NZ trade licences to be recognised in Australia. NOT automatic — must formally apply before starting work. NZ licence must be current. Apply state-by-state.
Automatic Mutual Recognition (AMR)
Applies to Australian state licences only — NOT NZ licences. Once a candidate has an Australian licence they can work in NSW, VIC, WA, SA, TAS, NT without re-applying. QLD does NOT participate in AMR. ACT excludes construction trades until July 2027.
